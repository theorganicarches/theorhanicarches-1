The Stain Journey

From Fresh Orange to Deep Maroon

Natural henna keeps developing after you remove the paste. Here's what to expect from our organic cones.

images/stain/stain-chart.jpg
Sample
images/stain/stain-progress.jpg
Sample

Just removed

A fresh, bright orange stain.

12–24 hours

The colour deepens to red, then maroon.

48 hours

The stain matures into a rich, dark shade.

6–10 days

It fades gradually — natural henna doesn't peel off.

For the darkest stain: keep the paste on 6–8 hours or overnight, avoid water for 2–3 hours after removing it, and moisturise. Thicker skin, like palms, stains darker.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers from our own care guides. Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

Slowly peel off the white backing paper, press the stencil firmly onto clean, dry skin and lift away the transparent top sheet. Fill the design with henna (two coats work best), remove the stencil as the henna dries, then let it dry and keep it on for 7–8 hours or overnight.

This depends on the design and how carefully it is removed. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you what to expect for the stencil you're choosing.

Yes. Our stencils are designed to make intricate patterns achievable even if you've never applied mehendi before — and our Kids stencils are sized for little hands.

Keep natural henna on for 6–8 hours, preferably overnight — the longer it stays, the darker the stain. The fresh stain is bright orange and deepens to a maroon shade over about 48 hours. Avoid water for 2–3 hours after removing the paste. A natural henna stain typically lasts 6–10 days and fades gradually rather than peeling off.

Scrape the dried paste off with your fingers or a blunt knife rather than washing it off, or use a little coconut or olive oil. Moisturising your stain helps it last longer, and keeping it away from water for as long as you can after removal gives a darker result.

Our cones are made with fresh, natural paste, so store them in the freezer when you're not using them. Nail cones keep fresh for up to 4–5 months in the freezer.
